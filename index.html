Our Mission

Building a Path to a Better Future

VOCES is a community-based initiative dedicated to empowering Latino families in the Twin Cities. We believe every person — regardless of background or status — deserves access to the resources, guidance, and support they need to build a stable and fulfilling life.

We are a small group of passionate individuals committed to building with purpose and intention, providing specialized services to help our community overcome barriers and take the next step toward a better future.

Community-Centered

Every decision made with our community's well-being at the center

Safe & Confidential

Your privacy and safety are our highest priority at every step

Bilingual Support

All services available in both English and Spanish
